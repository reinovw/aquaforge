FAQ

Good to know

Which prop is right for me?

Junior suits younger or developing swimmers who want to build technique without too much load. Standard is the original AquaForge prop, built for club-level training. Pro carries the most drag and suits competitive swimmers doing dedicated overload sessions.

Will it work with my stroke?

Yes — every tier is compatible with freestyle, backstroke, butterfly and breaststroke. The waist mount stays secure across all four.

Do I need my hands free for anything else?

No extra gear needed. Unlike hand paddles or tethers, the waist mount leaves your arms completely free for natural stroke mechanics.

Is delivery available outside major cities?

Yes, delivery is available across South Africa. Add your address at checkout and we'll take it from there.

Is the design protected?

Yes — the AquaForge propeller is a CIPC registered design.

How do I pay?

Checkout is handled securely by Yoco. You'll never enter card details on this site — Yoco's payment page handles that, and we only see the order once it's confirmed paid.
